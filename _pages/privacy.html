Privacy

This site counts visits with a small self-hosted counter. It sets no cookies and does not track you across sites. Your IP address is used once to look up an approximate city and network (via DB-IP) and is then discarded; it is never stored or logged. Visit records (time, page, referrer type, approximate location) are kept for 400 days. Browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are not counted by the in-page counter.
